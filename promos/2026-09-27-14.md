# Keeping Your AI Agents Local-First: The Shift Nobody Is Talking About

Build for the browser, keep context on your machine, and don't ship your data to a remote session.

At iGen we've been watching the agent tooling space closely all week. Two signals keep surfacing from independent builders: **"plan mode is dead"** — agents are now executing instead of proposing — and a growing wave of **local-first, privacy-first agent tools** (browser-based agents, no-SSH access, private journals with memory).

The uncomfortable part: when agents execute autonomously, the data they touch and the context they keep becomes your liability. That's why we just shipped two new products aimed at the gap between "agent hype" and "agent control":

1. **Local-First Agent Context Pack** ($14.99) — templates for context-passing, local memory schemas, and privacy-first routing rules so agents stay useful without shipping your context to a third party.
2. **AI Verification & Control Kit** ($24.99) — decision trees and review checklists for human-in-the-loop AI dev work: when to let the agent run, when to review, how to verify AI-written output.

Both are live at https://igen.tech and in the public catalog: https://github.com/bryantrudy24/iGen-Catalog

If you're running agents that write code, you're now the reviewer. The question isn't whether the agent can do the work — it's whether you can verify what it did. We're building for that question.
